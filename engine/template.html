      GSB Radar

      One minute, eight stops

      The tour walks through the page in the order you'd read it each morning: when it was updated, the three headline cards, your to-dos and assignments, the calendar, tags, and the two buttons that teach it what you like.

      Keyboard: → next · ← back · Esc leave.

      From your WhatsApp to this page

      What is read

      • WhatsApp group messages in the groups you tiered A, B or C (text and links; no photos)
      • If switched on: school email under your Gmail label, Canvas due dates from your calendar feed, your class and school calendars

      Never

      • Direct messages
      • Groups you put in D or X
      • Sending, replying, labelling — anything. The whole system is read-only.

      Where things live

      • ~/gsb-radar/me/ on your Mac: your settings, group tiers, ranking rules
      • Your Google Drive: the export bundle (overwritten each time)
      • This page's own database: check-offs, votes, past editions

      Timing: your Mac exports when WhatsApp syncs new messages (at most every 30 min) and uploads one file to your Drive; Claude rebuilds the page on its schedule (and right after each export if you turned on instant refresh). "Updated" at the top is the export time — anything posted after it shows up next time. No passwords or tokens exist anywhere in this setup; each step uses your own sign-ins.

      Every item gets a section and a priority

      Sections are by what you do with the item, not by which group it came from:

      • To do — something you can do now and check off, with a real deadline (forms, replies, prep). Event sign-ups only when registration is open.
      • Assignments — coursework, sorted by due date, with the course as a chip. Never mixed into To do.
      • About you — someone addressed you or needs your reply.
      • Go, join or sign up — you can attend, RSVP or join. Good to know — read and move on.
      • FYI strip — low priority; it exists so you know, nothing more.
      PriorityA deadline you own, something addressed to you, or a strong fit with the focus areas you named at setup. Used sparingly. NormalThe default. FYIThings you said are not for you, plus low-stakes chatter. Shown compact at the bottom.

      It learns from two buttons

      More like this / Not for me on any card mark it seen and are read on the next build. Two consistent votes on a kind of item become a standing rule. Checkmarks mean "handled", not "interested" — they don't change ranking. Items already on your own calendar are skipped on purpose; you see those on your calendar.

      Your standing rules live in plain English in me/prefs.md; learned rules appear under "About this edition" until you copy them in (next tab).

      Changing it is one sentence to your Claude

      Open the gsb-radar folder in Claude (desktop app with the folder connected, or Claude Code) and say what you want. It edits your settings and ships them on the next export. Some starters — tap Copy:
